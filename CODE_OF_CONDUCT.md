# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for all contributors and community members, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity, or sexual orientation.

## Expected Behavior

We expect all community members to:

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Listen to and value diverse perspectives
- Provide constructive feedback focused on ideas, not individuals
- Accept gracious criticism
- Focus on creating a positive community
- Show empathy toward other community members

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or discrimination based on protected characteristics
- Deliberate intimidation or bullying
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Hate speech or abusive language
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing private information without consent
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document the incident** - Note dates, times, and details
2. **Report privately** - Email conduct@ia-atomique.local or open a private issue
3. **Provide context** - Include relevant details without identifying individuals if possible

All reports will be:
- Reviewed promptly and fairly
- Treated confidentially
- Investigated thoroughly
- Result in appropriate action

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, pull requests)
- Offline interactions related to the project
- Representation of the project in public forums

## Consequences

Violations may result in:
- Warning from maintainers
- Temporary ban from participation
- Permanent removal from the community
